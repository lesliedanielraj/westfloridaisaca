How these numbers were made, and their limits
  • Everything comes from one export of the chapter's full member roster. It is a snapshot: it shows who is a member now, not who attends events or how membership changed over time.
  • Location uses each member's home ZIP code. Members with a home address outside Florida are counted in the totals but not shown on the map.
  • Job function and seniority are groupings of the professional area field, made for this dashboard. The original categories are shown in full under "Professional area".
  • Employer names are as members typed them, with common variants of the same name merged. Blank and placeholder entries are left out, and employers with fewer than three members in the group shown are not listed.
  • Shares are a percentage of all members on the roster or, when a seniority group is picked, of that group.
  • Seniority groups: C-suite and executives are the chief officer, VP and EVP categories; directors, managers and consultants are the categories named that way; staff and senior staff are the staff and auditor categories; students and academics are students, professors and teachers.
  • Small groups give small numbers. A chart for a group of a few dozen members is a count of a few people, not a trend.
